Figure 4. The labeling session and training loop. When a session ends, the post-session chain replays it, regenerates evidence and produces a matcher verdict; only the replay’s own fresh verdict can quarantine a session. In the labeling tool the builder then reviews one card per session: agreed sessions yield training pairs directly, contested sessions take the builder’s label as truth (“your word wins”), discarded sessions produce no belief pairs but still feed goal-free decoder pretraining, and quarantined or gate-failed sessions stay visible as read-only cards, excluded everywhere. The corpus currently holds 37 labeled real sessions (10 / 18 / 9). Pairs and goal-free rows form the training corpus — with a four-session pinned holdout — and every retrain re-runs the pre-registered standing monitors before anything ships.
