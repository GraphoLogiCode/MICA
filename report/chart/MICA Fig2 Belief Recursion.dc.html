Figure 2. The belief tracker’s predict→correct recursion. Between observed actions a Δt-aware CTMC kernel drifts the posterior bk−1(g, z); on each observed action the goal- and mode-conditioned likelihood P(ak | ek, g, z) multiplies in with an ε-floor and renormalisation, and the cycle advances the tick. Heads v1 are retrained on leak-free features and the regenerated evidence corpus; runtime asserts guard the likelihood range and belief positivity. Read-outs report the top goal, p*, entropy H(bk) and the deliberate-mode mass P(z=1). Belief confidence is not calibrated above 0.4 (an inversion), so confidence-licensed placement remains disabled until the Stage-1 recalibration (dashed, future); one-way constraints ①–③ mark where feedback is deliberately absent.
