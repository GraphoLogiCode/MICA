Figure 1. Overview of MICA, an intention-aware collaborative Minecraft agent. Each game tick, an observation packet splits into two parallel perception streams — 2D behaviour (frozen VPT and MineCLIP backbones with symbolic features) and 3D spatial (event-sourced voxel replay, per-goal template features, frozen Uni3D). A trained adapter Aφ fuses the evidence and two likelihood heads produce a goal- and mode-conditioned action likelihood P(ak | ek, g, z). A recursive belief tracker (highlighted; our contribution) maintains an explicit distribution bk(g, z) over five goals × two reasoning modes through a predict→correct rhythm. The belief licenses — but does not steer — a multi-token action decoder whose proposals pass a commit gate and safety FSM before an assistance action is emitted; placement can also be licensed by the consent route, where the agent voices an offer and the human’s “yes” licenses exactly one placement. Blue = belief path, green = anticipation and consent, grey = rule-based and frozen ❄ modules.
