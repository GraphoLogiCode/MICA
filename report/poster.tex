\begin{block}{Why this matters}
In structured work --- construction, warehouses, assembly --- an assistant that
must be \textbf{told everything is bottlenecked by the telling}. The scarce
resource is the human's attention. An assistant that \emph{infers} what you are
doing removes that bottleneck.\\[0.5em]
Assistance-game research formalized this (CIRL, 2016) and proved goal inference
is the load-bearing part: a \textbf{confidently wrong helper is worse than
none} (Watch-And-Help: 40\% of episodes, the helper undid the human's work).\\[0.5em]
\textbf{The gap:} every prior assistant reads oracle game state.
\textbf{Robots don't get oracle state.} MICA asks the question under robot
conditions --- perception in, calibration measured, authority earned.
\end{block}

\begin{block}{Technical method: the pipeline}
\begin{center}

\node[box, below=0.9cm of per] (bel) {\textbf{Belief} --- recursive Bayes $b(\text{goal},\text{mode})$\\

\node[box, below=0.9cm of bel] (dec) {\textbf{Decoder} --- 17.5M transformer, 87-token action grammar\\
  \scriptsize TRAINED: next-token warm-start $\to$ multi-token retrofit ($n{=}8$) ·

\node[box, below=0.9cm of dec] (gate) {\textbf{Safety gate + FSM} --- hand-coded, inspectable\\

\textbf{Trained}: likelihood heads + decoder (from my sessions).
\textbf{Frozen}: every foundation encoder --- models built to make agents

\textbf{Hand-coded}: everything that must be inspectable (gate, consent, rules).

\begin{alertblock}{What failed --- and why that is the point}
This project pre-registered its criteria and let them fire:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Early recognition: unproven.} The pre-registered early
    lock-in test fails for every arm ($p = 0.06$--$1.0$). Recognition is
    mid-build, not early --- more sessions are the path.
  \item \textbf{Confidence: not calibrated.} Above 0.4 stated confidence,
    held-out reliability \emph{inverts}. Consequence taken seriously: the
    confidence-licensed placement route is disabled by its own threshold.
  \item \textbf{The belief is not prediction fuel.} Forcing the belief into
    the action decoder changes nothing ($|\Delta| \le 0.001$ at every
    horizon) --- its value is \emph{judgment} (what to say, when to act),
    not forecasting.
  \item \textbf{The agent has never placed a block.} Zero, ever ---
    pinned. Assistance here is \emph{designed and safety-gated, never
    evaluated}. A helper that declines to act without license is the
    safety design working.
  \item \textbf{One verdict was reversed} --- by finding a feature leak,
    repairing the corpus, and re-running the pre-registered experiment
    (it then won on every criterion). The retraction is on the record.
\end{itemize}
\end{alertblock}

\begin{block}{What this means beyond Minecraft}
The approach transfers to any domain with: \textbf{(1)} discrete observable
actions, \textbf{(2)} goals legible from partial structure, \textbf{(3)}
cheap dense observation of a shared workspace --- bricklaying, timber
framing, warehouse picking, lab bench work.\\[0.5em]
\textbf{Next:} recalibration (the gate to autonomy) · consent-route sessions
with \emph{placement survival} as the outcome measure · more sessions for
the earliness question.
\end{block}

{\large\bfseries\color{micablue} METHOD --- how every number here was made trustworthy}\\[0.6em]
\begin{columns}[t]
\begin{column}{0.19\textwidth}
\textbf{Pre-registered.} Criteria, decision rules, and holdouts frozen and
dated \emph{before} every run --- and they fired: one verdict reversed, one
table retracted, failures banked.
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.19\textwidth}
\textbf{Known-truth anchored.} A scripted corpus whose labels are true by
construction: deterministic components are \emph{measured} (matcher 1.0),
never assumed.
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.19\textwidth}
\textbf{Leakage rules, standing.} Evidence strictly precedes the action it
scores; the agent's own blocks are never evidence (do-operator); violations
are asserts, not reviews.
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.19\textwidth}
\textbf{Controlled arms.} Four arms differ \emph{only} in the intent
mechanism --- same gate, same exposure-verified splits; experiments train to
separate files, production checksummed.
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.19\textwidth}
\textbf{One-command reproducible.} A single ledger-stamped cascade
regenerates every number on this poster from the raw recordings.
\end{column}
