\caption{\textbf{The decoder's domain gap, and closing it without labels.} The
action decoder trained only on scripted builds is 1.25 nats worse at predicting
a real human's next building action than a scripted one (gray). Pretraining its
next-token stage on real sessions' action streams---usable from \emph{every}
session, contested or discarded, because no goal label enters the sample---%
recovers most of the gap ($-37\%$ on held-out real sessions) while slightly
improving the scripted holdout. Pre-registered comparison, holdout sessions
fixed by id before the run.}

\caption{\textbf{The builder's word is good training data.} Training the
likelihood heads on matcher-contested sessions---with the builder's own label
as truth---improves every pre-registered criterion on a shared evaluation set
neither variant trained on: accuracy on both session groups, calibration
(ECE), and \emph{earliness} (sustained-from: the build-progress fraction from
which the top goal stays correct; lower = earlier). An identical experiment on
2026-07-12 found the opposite; the reversal followed the repair of a feature
leak and the evidence corpus, and is monitored at every retrain. Caveat: the
evaluation set is small (11 sessions).}
\end{figure}
